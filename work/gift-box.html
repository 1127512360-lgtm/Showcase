Background

设计背景

Marketing Design · 2024

Shuke holds roughly 5% of the electric oral-care market. Against the category leaders the brand has no differentiated memory point and has not yet established a category imprint in consumers’ minds — while domestic demand for electric toothbrushes is rising fast with living standards. That gap is the window.

Competitively: usmile has scale and iterates quickly; Philips carries old-brand authority in Germany; Laifen has proprietary technology paired with a visual style that reads like Apple and therefore more headroom on price. Competing head-on made no sense, so the plan was to flank with gifting — and to use an established workflow to test and land designs quickly, then amplify them with each marketing node.

On the consumer side, the mainstream buyer is Gen Z, whose needs concentrate on emotional value and social currency; “looks good” and “good for gifting” are a large share of review keywords. So illustration and IP collaborations became the fast route to emotional value, while SKUs gained couple colourways to serve gifting and lift the two-unit purchase rate.

舒客电动口腔产品市场占比在 5% 左右，与头部品牌相比缺少差异化记忆点，尚未在消费者心中建立品类印记；而随着生活品质需求攀升，国内电动牙刷消费市场正在迅猛增长 —— 这是抢占消费者关注度与市场份额的窗口。

竞争分析：usmile 具有规模优势、新品迭代快；飞利浦作为德国老牌权威，品牌知名度高；徕芬的独家技术与高度对齐苹果的视觉风格带来更大溢价空间。正面硬拼不划算，于是选择用礼盒礼赠创意侧面突围，并用成熟的工作流快速测试与落地设计，配合营销节点放大。

消费者分析：电子消费品主流人群是 Z 世代，核心需求聚焦情绪价值与社交属性，商品评论中「颜值」「送礼」关键词占比较大。日常以插画与 IP 合作为切入点快速满足情绪价值与社交属性；SKU 加强情侣色搭配，满足送礼需求的同时提升双支率。

Approach

设计思路

3 decisions

Festival Gift Sets — the decisions that set the direction. 舒客礼盒项目 —— 决定方向的几个选择。

  1. 01

    Flank with gifting侧面突围：礼盒礼赠

    Instead of competing on price against the category leaders, the campaign enters through the gifting occasion where the brand can define the rules.

    不与头部品牌正面比价，用礼盒礼赠创意切入送礼场景，在品牌可以定义规则的场合竞争。

  2. 02

    Illustration and IP for emotional value插画与 IP 满足情绪价值

    Everyday communication uses illustration and IP collaborations so emotional value and social currency are satisfied quickly — and cheaply.

    日常以插画与 IP 合作切入，快速且低成本地满足 Z 世代的情绪价值与社交属性。

  3. 03

    Couple colourways to lift the pair rate情侣色搭配提升双支率

    SKUs gained couple colour pairings, which serves the gifting occasion and raises the two-unit purchase rate in the same move.

    SKU 加强情侣色搭配，既满足送礼需求，又同时提升双支率。
