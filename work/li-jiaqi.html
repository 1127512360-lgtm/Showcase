Background

设计背景

Marketing Design · 2024

The opening slot on Li Jiaqi’s Double 11 peak day was won through the variety show All Girls’ Offer — a chance to put the brand inside the vocabulary of the platform’s biggest livestream. The visuals had to serve three places at once: the show, the stream and the product listing.

One key visual covers show exposure, livestream graphics, the creator listing image and co-branded packaging, so an audience moving from programme to stream to product page keeps seeing the same Shuke. Co-branded packaging with Candy Land carries that attention back to the product itself.

通过综艺《所有女生的 OFFER》拿下李佳琦双 11 爆发日首日坑位 —— 这是把品牌放进头部直播间话语体系里的机会，视觉必须同时服务综艺场景、直播画面与商品链接。

一套 KV 覆盖综艺露出、直播间贴片、达人链接主图与联名包装，让观众从节目到直播、再到商品页看到同一个舒客；与 Candy Land 的联名包装与周边把直播间的注意力带回产品本身。

Approach

设计思路

3 decisions

Livestream Collaboration — the decisions that set the direction. 李佳琦合作项目 —— 决定方向的几个选择。

  1. 01

    One key visual across show and stream节目与直播共用一套 KV

    Show exposure, livestream graphics and the creator listing image share one visual system, which removed most of the late-stage rework.

    综艺露出、直播间贴片与达人链接主图共用同一套视觉，大幅减少上线前的临时改稿。

  2. 02

    Co-branded packaging to catch the traffic联名包装承接流量

    Co-branded packaging and merchandise with Candy Land turn livestream attention into something the viewer can actually hold.

    与 Candy Land 的联名包装与周边，把直播间的注意力变成观众真正能拿到手的东西。

  3. 03

    Amplify the opening slot用首日坑位放大节点

    Assets were released against the Double 11 peak-day opening slot and the pre-sale rhythm, concentrating the spend where it can compound.

    素材围绕双 11 爆发日首日坑位与预售节奏集中释放，让投入在能被放大的时点生效。
