Background

设计背景

Marketing Design · 2024 — 2025

The youth-ambassador campaign runs on a single line — “朝朝「穆穆」，舒展笑颜” — the ambassador’s name folded into the headline so one phrase carries both the brand’s tone of voice and the campaign’s memory hook.

Out of that line came a portrait set in two directions: a studio look for clean cut-outs and a campaign look for the announcement. The electric brush, toothpaste and gift box sit in frame with him, so the same artwork can carry product claims into retail as well as into feeds.

青春代言人 campaign 的主线是一句话：「朝朝『穆穆』舒展笑颜」—— 把代言人的名字嵌进主标语，让一句祝福同时承担品牌语气与传播记忆点。

围绕这条主线产出一套人像素材：一种棚拍造型用于干净抠图，一种传播造型用于官宣画面；电动牙刷、牙膏与礼盒与代言人同框，让同一张物料既能进信息流，也能承接产品卖点进入终端。

Approach

设计思路

3 decisions

Youth Brand Ambassador · Shuke — the decisions that set the direction. 舒客青春代言人 —— 决定方向的几个选择。

  1. 01

    Put the name in the headline把代言人的名字写进主标语

    “朝朝「穆穆」，舒展笑颜” folds MZC’s name into the campaign line, so the ambassador and the slogan are the same object — nothing has to be explained twice.

    「朝朝『穆穆』舒展笑颜」把穆祉丞的名字嵌进主标语，代言人与广告语合二为一，不需要再解释一次。

  2. 02

    One portrait set, two jobs一套素材，两种用途

    A studio look gives clean cut-outs for standees and listing images; a campaign look holds the announcement. Both come from one shoot, so the campaign and the retail shelf stay in the same frame of reference.

    棚拍造型提供干净抠图，用于立牌与商品图；传播造型承接官宣画面。两种素材来自同一次拍摄，让 campaign 与货架保持同一套视觉参照。

  3. 03

    Product in the frame让产品与代言人同框

    Rather than an empty portrait, the brush, toothpaste and gift box share the frame — the poster sells the person and the product without a second layout.

    画面没有留成单纯的肖像，而是让电动牙刷、牙膏与礼盒同框 —— 一张海报同时完成代言与卖货，不必再做一版。
