Background

设计背景

Product Visual Design · 2024

The router needed to make Wi-Fi 6 legible on one screen and be compared directly with the rest of the category, where “more performance” and “real materials” are the two claims that actually move a buyer.

The direction is restrained, technological and professional: centred first-screen measure, card-based split screens for the claims, left-aligned text. Four-antenna build, full dual-band Wi-Fi 6 and one-tap whole-home coverage each get a fixed place in the layout.

新品路由器需要在同一屏里讲清 Wi-Fi 6 的能力，并直接与市场同类型竞品做对比 —— 性能升级与「真材实料」是这一代最主要的两个差异点。

整体基调是精炼简洁、科技感、专业感：首屏版心居中，用卡片式分屏承载卖点，文字左对齐。加粗 4 天线、真·双频满血 Wi-Fi 6、一键扩展全屋 Wi-Fi 等卖点按重要度排进固定版式。

Approach

设计思路

3 decisions

Router Launch Page — the decisions that set the direction. Wi-Fi 6 路由器 —— 决定方向的几个选择。

  1. 01

    Open with a line people can repeat用一句能被记住的话开场

    The 85pt headline “新晋顶 6 踢馆” sets the product’s attitude before any specification appears.

    大标题 85pt「新晋顶 6 踢馆」，在产品参数出现之前先把态度立住。

  2. 02

    Card split to carry the claims卡片式分屏承载卖点

    Page title 60pt “加粗 4 天线 高能信号塔”, mid headline 48pt “真·双频满血 Wi-Fi 6”, sub 36pt “一键扩展全屋 Wi-Fi”, body 28pt for function detail such as whitelists and parental time limits.

    页面标题 60pt「加粗 4 天线 高能信号塔」、中标题 48pt「真·双频满血 Wi-Fi 6」、小标题 36pt「一键扩展全屋 Wi-Fi」，正文 28pt 承接功能细节（如设置白名单、管理孩子上网时间）。

  3. 03

    Built for feature-first buyers, 25–48面向 25–48 岁功能优先人群

    Footnotes sit at a uniform 16pt and always name the test source, so performance claims stay credible with an audience that reads the small print.

    注释统一 16pt 并标注测试来源，让性能主张在会看小字的专业人群面前依然可信。
