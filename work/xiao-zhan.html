Background

设计背景

Marketing Design · 2024

The point of the ambassador project is not producing one key visual. It is turning an announcement into a complete chain: participating in the full campaign plan, coordinating resources and timelines, and laying out the route from objective breakdown to rollout.

On the content side I led creative brainstorm and visual design, producing the full set from KV concept to detail, then took it through internal presentation and decision. On the execution side I managed external suppliers alongside internal teams, supervising production, build, photography and retouching end to end — and handling crises when they came.

代言人项目的核心不是出一张 KV，而是把一次官宣变成一条完整链路：参与制定全案企划，统筹资源与时间线，完成从目标拆解到落地路径的整体规划。

内容端主导创意脑暴与视觉设计，产出从 KV 概念到细节的全套设计方案，并完成内部提报与决策；执行端管理外部供应商与内部协作，监督制作、搭建、摄影后期等全流程，确保方案精准执行并具备危机应对能力。
